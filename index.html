Computational Radiation Physicist with experience at CERN, TRIUMF, and Brookhaven National Laboratory. Extensive expertise in Monte Carlo radiation transport across a broad range of particle species, energies, and applications—from high-energy accelerators and colliders to medical facilities. Strong background in nuclear-data processing, shielding analysis, scientific code development, and high-performance and high-throughput computing (HPC/HTC), complemented by a solid foundation in machine learning. Experience with experimental and clinical beams informs this computational work.
